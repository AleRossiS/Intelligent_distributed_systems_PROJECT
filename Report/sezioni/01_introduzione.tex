\noindent This project presents a control framework for a swarm of mobile robots that find, estimates and aligns around a target. The system combines a lidar, for obstacle detection, an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) for state estimation and linear consensus for coordination. The robots are able to estimate the position of the target using Angle of Arrival (known also as Bearing angles). The detection of the target is performed by a simple thresholding of the received signal strength emitted by the treasure, which is assumed to be a weak radio emitter. The robots are able to coordinate their movements using a linear consensus algorithm, which allows them to converge to the estimated position of the target while avoiding obstacles. The performance of the proposed control framework is evaluated through simulations in a 2D environment, where the robots are tasked with finding and aligning around a target while navigating through a cluttered environment. The results show that the proposed approach is effective in enabling the swarm of robots to find and align around the target, even in the presence of noise and uncertainties in the environment.
